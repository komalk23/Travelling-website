  • Udaipur, Rajasthan — The City of Lakes

Udaipur is one of the most beautiful cities in Rajasthan, famous for its stunning lakes, royal palaces, and rich cultural heritage. Surrounded by the Aravalli Hills, the city offers breathtaking views and a peaceful atmosphere. Visitors can enjoy boat rides on Lake Pichola and explore the magnificent City Palace.

Famous Places:City Palace, Lake Pichola, Fateh Sagar Lake, Sajjangarh Monsoon Palace.

  • Agra, Uttar Pradesh — The City of the Taj Mahal

Agra is a historic city famous for the magnificent Taj Mahal, one of the Seven Wonders of the World. The city showcases beautiful Mughal architecture, rich history, and impressive monuments. It is a perfect destination for travellers who love history, art, and architecture. .

Famous Places:Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Fatehpur Sikri.

  • Manali, Himachal Pradesh — The Mountain Paradise

Manali is a beautiful hill station nestled in the Himalayas, known for its snow-covered mountains, green valleys, and flowing rivers. It is an ideal destination for nature lovers who want to enjoy peaceful surroundings and breathtaking landscapes. Visitors can explore charming villages and experience the beauty of the mountains. .

Famous Places:Solang Valley, Hadimba Temple, Old Manali, Rohtang Pass.

  • Goa — The Beach Paradise

Goa is a popular tourist destination famous for its golden beaches, swaying palm trees, and vibrant culture. The state offers a wonderful combination of natural beauty, Portuguese heritage, colourful markets, and delicious local cuisine. Visitors can relax by the sea, explore historic churches, and enjoy beautiful sunsets.

Famous Places:Baga Beach, Calangute Beach, Fort Aguada, Basilica of Bom Jesus.

  • Srinagar, Jammu and Kashmir — Heaven on Earth

Srinagar is a breathtaking destination known for its beautiful lakes, colourful gardens, and majestic mountain views. Dal Lake, with its traditional houseboats and shikaras, is one of the city's most famous attractions. The peaceful surroundings and natural beauty make Srinagar a memorable place to explore.

Famous Places:Dal Lake, Mughal Gardens, Indira Gandhi Memorial Tulip Garden, Shankaracharya Temple.

  • Jaipur, Rajasthan — The Pink City

Jaipur is the capital of Rajasthan and is famous for its pink-coloured buildings, magnificent forts, and royal palaces. The city reflects the grandeur of Rajasthan's royal history through its impressive architecture and traditional culture. Visitors can explore colourful markets, discover local handicrafts, and enjoy delicious Rajasthani food.

Famous Places:Hawa Mahal, Amer Fort, City Palace, Jantar Mantar.
